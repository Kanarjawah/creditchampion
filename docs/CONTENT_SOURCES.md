# Editorial sources and maintenance

Source pages reviewed November 13, 2026 (America/Phoenix). Review means the cited official page was consulted for educational summaries or directory placement; it does not verify every eligibility criterion, legal effective date or present availability. Articles expose source links and a professional-review-pending label. Financial source data expires into review after 90 days. Loan figures are user-supplied illustrations, not current offers. No mileage rates, filing fees, debt thresholds or tax deadlines are hardcoded.

The complete linked source registry is in `src/knowledge.ts` and each provider source is in `src/funding-data.ts`.

- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: credit building, rebuilding, factual disputes, accurate negatives and vehicle repossession.
- U.S. Courts: Chapter 7, Chapter 13 and filing without an attorney.
- U.S. Department of Justice: approved prefiling credit counseling agencies.
- IRS: small-business tax center, Publication 583 and Topic 510 business vehicle use.
- SBA: loan programs, Lender Match and grant restrictions.
- Grants.gov: official opportunity search; no open grant inventory was imported.
- Treasury CDFI Fund and NCUA: institution discovery.
- Bank of America, Ford Pro and GM Financial: business vehicle financing source pages. U.S. Bank, Chase, Wells Fargo, LiftFund and DreamSpring: product discovery pages. Complete provider criteria and pricing require direct review.
- FTC/CFR regulatory sources: registry entries for professional applicability review, not a determination that the app complies with every rule.

Before publishing a change, recheck official sources, avoid unsourced numerical claims, log the editorial review date, and flag unresolved legal/tax questions. Do not promise removal of accurate negative reporting, conceal liability behind a business name, invent identity theft, encourage false applications or describe an ordinary lender directory as a grant award.
